The Programme

Closing the Loop on Farm Waste

We work directly with farms to intercept organic waste before it goes to landfill.

🌾

Farm Lists Surplus

Farms submit their organic waste and surplus produce directly through our platform.

→
🔄

We Sort & Process

Edible surplus becomes discounted veg boxes. Non-edible waste is composted or turned into fertiliser.

→
🌱

Community Benefits

Users get affordable organic veg. Schools and gardens receive free compost. Farms avoid disposal costs.

What We Collect

Two streams, one solution

🥦

Edible Surplus

Oddly shaped vegetables, cosmetically imperfect produce, excess harvest — perfectly good food that would be discarded purely for looks or volume.

  • ✅ Packed into affordable veg boxes
  • ✅ Listed on Birp at 40–60% discount
  • ✅ Delivered or collected from the farm
Browse Veg Boxes
🍂

Organic Waste

Peels, husks, trimmings and non-edible plant matter. Instead of going to landfill, this becomes something valuable for communities.

  • ♻️ Composted into rich garden compost
  • 🌿 Processed into organic fertiliser
  • 🏫 Distributed free to schools & community gardens
