Why It Matters

South Africa wastes 10 million tonnes of food every year.

That's food that could feed millions of people. Most of it ends up in landfill, releasing methane and contributing to climate change. Birp connects that surplus food directly to people who want it — at prices that work for everyone.

  • ✅ Reduces greenhouse gas emissions
  • ✅ Saves consumers up to 70% on meals
  • ✅ Helps businesses recover costs on unsold stock
  • ✅ Turns organic waste into compost & fertiliser
