    A school project that became a real mission.

    We're a group of grade 9 students at Bishops (Diocesan College) in Cape Town. What started as a philanthropy assignment turned into something we genuinely care about — fighting food waste in South Africa.

    How It Started

    The Problem We Couldn't Ignore

    In our philanthropy class, we were challenged to identify a real-world problem and design a solution. We kept coming back to food waste — a crisis hiding in plain sight.

    Every day, tonnes of perfectly good food gets thrown away by restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores and farms — while millions of South Africans struggle with food insecurity. That disconnect felt unacceptable to us.

    We researched businesses like Too Good To Go, Refreshi and Food Forward SA — and asked: what if we built something like this, designed specifically for our communities?

    Birp was born from that question.

    "We couldn't look at the numbers and do nothing. 10 million tonnes wasted while people go hungry — that's a solvable problem."
    — The Birp Team
    10M+ tonnes wasted in SA yearly
    1 in 5 South Africans food insecure

    Our Inspiration

    Standing on the shoulders of giants

    We were inspired by businesses already fighting food waste — and we aim to bring their energy to our communities.

    🇿🇦

    Refreshi

    Cape Town-based platform connecting consumers with surplus meals from local businesses. Our closest inspiration for the South African context.

    🌍

    Too Good To Go

    Global leader in food waste reduction. Their surprise bag model — buy a mystery bundle at a fraction of retail — inspired our core offering.

    💚

    Food Forward SA

    South African NPO tackling food insecurity by rescuing fresh produce. Their farm focus inspired our organic waste and compost programme.
